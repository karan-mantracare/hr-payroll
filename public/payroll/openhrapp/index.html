OpenHRApp

Open Source HR & Workforce Management

OpenHRApp is an open-source HR and workforce platform designed for lean, high-trust teams. It provides modern employee self-service, shift scheduling, real-time attendance, and automated leave management.

Open live app ↗ View source code ↗
⏱️ Attendance & Shifts

Automated shift rosters, real-time clock-in and clock-out tracking, break management, and attendance regularization requests for remote and in-office staff.

🏖️ Leave Requests & Approvals

Flexible leave balance policies, multi-tier manager approvals, status notifications, and comprehensive leave tracking with automated accruals.

👥 Employee Directory

Centralized workforce directory, department groupings, reporting lines, contact details, and self-service profile management for employees and HR admins.

🎯 Performance Reviews

Structured 1-on-1 reviews, continuous feedback cycles, team goal setting, and milestone tracking aligned with company objectives.

📊 Analytics & Reporting

Comprehensive headcount metrics, department breakdowns, attendance summaries, and attrition reporting for leadership oversight.

🔐 Self-Hosted & Cloud Ready

Independent authentication and tenant isolation. Sign in directly with your OpenHRApp credentials or deploy your own dedicated instance.

Payroll Integration & Data Export
OpenHRApp focuses on core workforce management and attendance. It exports structured timesheet logs, approved leaves, and verified work hours in standard formats (CSV/JSON/API) for seamless integration with enterprise payroll engines.
